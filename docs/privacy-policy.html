Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

At a glance

BLACKLINE: Crime Simulation is an offline, single-player management RPG. Your story, settings and progress are stored locally on your device. Optional Google services are used only when you choose to load an advertisement, manage privacy choices, or purchase Remove Ads.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your save, settings, accessibility preferences, ad entitlement, and a small amount of gameplay state in local app storage. This information is used to resume your story, apply settings, remember a completed purchase, and enforce the one rewarded-supply claim per game day. We do not operate a game account or a game backend.

Advertising and consent

With your consent where required, the Android build can request banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertising through Google Mobile Ads. Google may process device and network information, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics, and interaction data to deliver, measure, protect, and improve advertising. Google’s consent form and privacy options are presented through the Google User Messaging Platform when applicable.

Rewarded advertising is optional. A completed rewarded view grants the single daily supply benefit shown in the game. Remove Ads is a one-time Google Play purchase that removes the three ad formats and provides the equivalent daily supply option without a video.

Google Play purchases

If you buy Remove Ads, Google Play processes the transaction. BLACKLINE receives the purchase state and product entitlement needed to grant the feature; it does not receive or store your full payment card number. Google Play may retain transaction information under its own privacy policy. The product identifier is remove_ads.

Sharing and disclosure

There is no BLACKLINE account system and no sale of personal information. The app does not upload your local save to a BLACKLINE server. Google Mobile Ads, Google consent services, and Google Play Billing receive information necessary for their services when those services are used. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the service.

Children

BLACKLINE is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Do not use the app if you are not permitted to access fictional crime-themed content in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can play offline, disable sound and ambience, and manage privacy choices in the game’s Settings when Google provides a privacy-options form. You can reset or delete local progress from Settings. You can uninstall the app to remove its local storage. Google advertising and purchase controls are also available through your Google account and device settings.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, Google services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected in the app listing or release notes where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the public project contact channel at github.com/fareza777/Crime-simulation-GPT61/issues. Please do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
